## How it works

**1. Publish a task** &nbsp;—&nbsp; Star a GitHub repo, upvote on Product Hunt, or verify via webhook. Takes under a minute to set up.

**2. Other founders complete your tasks** &nbsp;—&nbsp; Every action is server-side verified. No fake clicks, no gaming the system.

**3. Karma drives your visibility** &nbsp;—&nbsp; High-karma contributors get prioritized in the feed. No ad budget needed.

**4. Reciprocity is enforced** &nbsp;—&nbsp; If you owe actions to other users, your feed locks to only tasks that satisfy those obligations until they are cleared.

## Why it works

- **Real testing actions, not vanity metrics.** Every task produces genuine signal for your product.

- **Fair visibility driven by contribution, not budget.** Small teams compete on equal footing.
